Effective from 22 September 2026

Who processes your data

The data controller is Shades lounge bar, s.r.o., Company ID (IČO) 23660635, registered at Nádražní 2/1, 682 01 Vyškov, Czech Republic (the “operator”).

Contact for data protection matters: phone +420 731 050 372.

What data we process

  • Reservations and catering — if you book a table or request catering by phone, we process your name, phone number and the details of the booking.
  • Social media messages — if you write to us on Instagram, we process the content of your message and your profile name.
  • CCTV — where in operation on the premises, it is announced by a notice at the entrance.

Why we process it

Reservations and enquiries are processed in order to enter into and perform a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). CCTV and property protection rely on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data on tax documents is kept because the law requires it (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

How long we keep it

Reservation data is deleted after your visit, at the latest within three months. Accounting and tax documents are kept for the period required by law, usually 10 years. CCTV recordings are overwritten automatically, typically within 14 days.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data or pass it to third parties for marketing purposes. Access is limited to our staff, our accountant and our web hosting provider, and only to the extent necessary.

Cookies and this website

This website uses no analytics or advertising cookies and does not track your behaviour. The Google Maps map is loaded only after you click it — only then may Google set cookies under the Google Privacy Policy. Links to Instagram and Linktree likewise lead to third-party services with their own rules.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Just call us on +420 731 050 372.

If you believe we handle your data incorrectly, you may lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7.
